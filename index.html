Privacy Policy

Spore Runner · Effective date: 15 May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we handles information when you use Spore Runner (the “App”), including the Android application (package name com.ghostjump.spore) and any web version of the game we make available.

By using the App, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Summary

  • We do not require an account, and we do not operate a login system.
  • We do not knowingly collect your name, email, or postal address through the game itself.
  • Some game settings and scores are stored only on your device.
  • The Android App shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect data for advertising and measurement.
  • If you play a hosted web version, we may use privacy-oriented analytics to understand aggregate usage.

2. Information stored on your device

The App uses your device’s local storage (for example, browser localStorage on web, or equivalent storage in the mobile app) to remember:

  • High scores (separate records for each flight mode)
  • Your selected flight mode (Side Scroll or Climb Mode)
  • Whether sound is muted

This information stays on your device unless you clear app data, uninstall the App, or clear your browser storage. We do not receive this data on our own servers.

3. Advertising (Android App)

The Android version of Spore Runner displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC), including banner ads and full-screen interstitial ads (for example, after a game ends).

AdMob and its partners may collect or receive information such as:

  • Advertising ID (AAID on Android)
  • Device type, operating system version, and language
  • IP address and general location (country/region level)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

Where required by law, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent for personalized advertising and related processing. You can change your choices later in your device settings and, where available, through in-app privacy or consent options.

To limit personalized ads on Android: open Settings → Google → Ads and enable “Opt out of Ads Personalization” (wording may vary by device).

Learn more about how Google uses data: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

4. Analytics (web version)

If you access Spore Runner through a website we host in production, we may use Vercel Web Analytics (Vercel Inc.) to collect aggregated, non-identifying usage information (such as page views and performance metrics). This helps us understand how the game is used and improve stability.

Vercel’s privacy information: Vercel Privacy Policy.

The standalone Android App is not designed to require a separate analytics account from us; however, third-party ad SDKs (see Section 3) may still collect data as described above.

5. Fonts and other third-party services

The App may load fonts or other assets from third-party providers (for example, Google Fonts). Those providers may receive technical data such as your IP address when your device requests those resources.

6. What we do not collect

Through the game itself, we do not intentionally collect:

  • Real name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Payment or financial information (the App is free to play; ads are served by Google)

7. Children’s privacy

Spore Runner is a casual game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at eagleye613@gmail.com and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

The App may show ads. Parents and guardians should review ad settings on the child’s device.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on:

  • Consent — for personalized advertising where UMP or similar consent is shown
